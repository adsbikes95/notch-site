Privacy policy

Last updated November 15, 2026

Notch Dock runs on your PC. It has no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and the developer never receives your data. Everything the app shows is read on your PC and kept there, except for the few lookups below, which go straight from your PC to public services so the app can show weather, lyrics and cover art, and the questions you choose to send to an AI service from the Ask tab.

Notch Dock does not send them your name, email, files or clipboard, apart from anything you type or paste into the Ask tab yourself. Purchases of Notch Pro are handled by the Microsoft Store, and the developer only sees totals that Microsoft reports.

Your controls

Every feature has its own switch in Settings, so you can turn off clipboard history, notifications, calendar, lyrics, weather, the camera button, the Ask tab or Claude Code at any time. You can also withdraw location permission in Windows Settings, which only hides Wi-Fi names in the Wi-Fi list. Clear clipboard and notifications from their tabs. Uninstalling the app removes its folder and everything in it.

Children

Notch Dock does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes and questions

If a new feature changes what the app reads or sends, this page will be updated before that version ships. Questions go through the support page.
