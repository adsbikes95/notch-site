Support

Found a bug or have an idea? Open an issue on GitHub and include your Windows version and what you were doing. Replies usually come within a few days.

Common questions

How do I open the notch? Hover over the pill at the top of the screen, scroll on it, or press the shortcut set in Settings (Alt+N by default).

My music doesn't show. Notch Dock works with players that report to Windows, like Spotify, Apple Music and browsers, plus Qobuz. Start playback once after opening Notch Dock.

Brightness sliders are missing. External monitors need DDC/CI turned on in the monitor's own menu.

Notch Dock doesn't start with Windows. Turn on Start at login in Settings, and check that Notch Dock is allowed under Windows Settings, Apps, Startup.

I bought Pro on another PC. Sign in to the Microsoft Store with the same Microsoft account and Pro unlocks automatically.

How do I get a refund? Refunds for Notch Pro go through Microsoft's order history at account.microsoft.com.

Notch Dock is an independent app and is not affiliated with Apple or Microsoft.
