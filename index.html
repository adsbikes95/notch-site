Notch Dock

A pull-down dock for the top of your Windows screen.

Notch Dock stays a small pill until you hover over it, scroll on it or press a shortcut. Then it opens to show your music, weather, calendar, timers, clipboard and a file shelf, and tucks away again when you move on.

The app is free. Notch Pro is a one-time purchase in the app that unlocks extra styles, synced lyrics, monitor brightness, shelf actions and more. There is no subscription, no account, no ads and no tracking.

Get it from the Microsoft Store (link coming at launch).

Notch Dock is an independent app and is not affiliated with Apple or Microsoft.
